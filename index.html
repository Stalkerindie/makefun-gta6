NEON HARBOR

Marlowe Bay. One driver. Fifty grand of debt.

▶ Play Neon Harbor

Runs entirely in your browser — no downloads, no accounts, no network.

The game

  • An open city to drive and walk: traffic, pedestrians, parks, the docks, and the bay.
  • Six story jobs for fixer Mara Voss — steal a car, run a parcel, tail a courier, lose the heat, crack the bank vault, and make the last run to the Old Pier.
  • A wanted system from 0 to 5 stars, with Harbor Police cars and foot cops that chase, ram, and arrest.
  • A living minimap with the objective, police, cash and repair toolboxes — plus an edge arrow that points at the job when it is off-screen.
  • Day and night: headlights cut through the dark, and repair toolboxes patch a busted car back up.
  • Synthesized WebAudio sound, a best-haul record, and a save that keeps your cash and progress.

Controls

  • WASD or arrows — move and drive
  • E — steal or leave a car
  • Space (hold to keep firing) or click — shoot
  • P — pause
  • On phones and tablets: a joystick bottom-left, and buttons bottom-right to enter a car, shoot, pause, and confirm.
